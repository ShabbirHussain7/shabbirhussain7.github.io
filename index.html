About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Roya Ensafi as part of Censored Planet. My research is in network security and privacy: I measure how Internet users are censored and tracked, and build systems that help defend against it. Most recently I have worked on how censors detect circumvention tools from the shape of their traffic. My earlier research studied web tracking and misconfigurations in cloud applications.

Before joining Michigan in 2025, I received my BS in Computer Science from the Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS).
